Sprint 4.5 Housekeeping Step 4B - Output Cleanup

No runtime source files need manual deletion after extraction.

On the first fresh Knowledge Base Population run, PDC automatically removes
superseded timestamped population reports such as:

    KB_POPULATION_YYYYMMDD_HHMMSS__RESULTS.csv
    KB_POPULATION_YYYYMMDD_HHMMSS__FAILURES.csv
    KB_POPULATION_YYYYMMDD_HHMMSS__SKIPPED.csv
    KB_POPULATION_YYYYMMDD_HHMMSS__CANDIDATES.csv
    KB_POPULATION_YYYYMMDD_HHMMSS__PROVIDER_ERRORS.csv
    KB_POPULATION_YYYYMMDD_HHMMSS__SUMMARY.json
    KB_POPULATION_YYYYMMDD_HHMMSS__RUN_LOG.txt

PDC deliberately does NOT auto-delete human-edited:

    * __CANDIDATE_REVIEW.csv

For this development repository, once you have confirmed that the decisions in
an old dated Candidate Review have already been promoted into the committed
KNOWLEDGE_HISTORY, you may delete that old dated review file manually. Future
Candidate Reviews use the fixed filename:

    KB_POPULATION__CANDIDATE_REVIEW.csv
