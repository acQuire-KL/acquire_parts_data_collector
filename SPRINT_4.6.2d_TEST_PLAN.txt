SPRINT 4.6.2d — CONTROLLED ENGINEERING ACTION HANDOFF

Acceptance checks:
1. Only a specific Accepted candidate review can be handed off.
2. Rejected/Pending/Needs Verification candidates cannot be handed off.
3. Current-state bulk handoff excludes stale historical Accepted decisions.
4. Explicit context can propose AVL Addition, BOM Change, temporary
   Concession/Deviation, or Reference Only.
5. Missing/ambiguous context is not guessed; it becomes Needs Engineering
   Classification.
6. Handoff preserves candidate/review evidence and warnings.
7. Proposal persistence is append-only.
8. Review history is not mutated by handoff.
9. No Parts Master, BOM, AVL, AIPN, ECO or production-authorisation change
   occurs.
10. No provider/API call occurs.
